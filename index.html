GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can SafeCheck guarantee a link is safe?

No. No single checker can guarantee a website or message is safe. A low score is not proof of legitimacy.

Does this send my message to a server?

Message and email text are analyzed locally in your browser. If live URL checking is enabled, a URL may be sent to the reputation service. Do not paste passwords, payment details, or verification codes.

Does SafeCheck check live scam databases?

Local warning-pattern analysis is available. Live URL reputation checks are not currently enabled.

What should I do with a suspicious message?

Do not click its links or call its numbers. Contact the company through an official channel you find independently.
