GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can SafeCheck guarantee a link is safe?

No. No single checker can guarantee a website or message is safe. A clean result is not proof of legitimacy.

Does this preview send my message to a server?

No. Pattern checks run locally in your browser. Do not paste passwords, payment details, or verification codes.

Is the full AI checker available yet?

Not yet. Live AI analysis, external URL reputation checks, screenshot analysis, accounts, and paid plans are not connected.

What should I do with a suspicious message?

Do not click its links or call its numbers. Contact the company through an official channel you find independently.
