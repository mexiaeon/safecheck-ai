# SafeCheck AI Privacy Policy **Effective date: November 25, 2026** SafeCheck AI helps people review suspicious messages and website addresses for common scam warning signs. This policy explains what information the site processes and when information may be sent to another service. ## 1. Messages and email text When you paste a text message or email and choose **Analyze warning signs**, the current checker analyzes that text in your browser using built-in rules. - The message or email text is not sent to the SafeCheck AI website server for this local analysis. - The current checker does not save your pasted message or email to a SafeCheck account or database. - Avoid pasting passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal information. ## 2. Website addresses and live reputation checks The checker analyzes a pasted website address in your browser for visible URL patterns, such as lookalike domains, shortened links, and unusual address structures. **Live reputation lookup is currently disabled.** The site may contact its Cloudflare Worker health endpoint to check whether live lookup has been configured. That health request does not include the message or website address you entered. If live URL reputation lookup is enabled in the future, the URL you ask to check will be sent to the SafeCheck AI Cloudflare Worker and then to Google Web Risk for a reputation check. Do not submit private, personal, or access-controlled links. ## 3. Hosting and service providers SafeCheck AI is hosted using GitHub Pages. The site also uses a Cloudflare Worker for its live-check configuration and, if enabled, URL-checking functions. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request logs when your browser connects to their services. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. - [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) - [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) ## 4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising The current SafeCheck AI site does not intentionally set advertising cookies, run analytics tools, or display personalized advertising. This may change if analytics or advertising services are added. If that happens, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant services and choices. ## 5. Third-party links The site may link to external resources, such as the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's scam guidance. When you follow an external link, that website's privacy policy applies. ## 6. Children's privacy SafeCheck AI is a general information tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit another person's private information. ## 7. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated when the site's features or service providers change. The effective date at the top will be revised when an update is made. ## 8. Contact A dedicated privacy contact address has not yet been published. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the website.
